Privacy Policy

Paws of War · Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Paws of War ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information. The Game is published by N Kusmuk.

In short: the Game does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It works entirely on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game has no user accounts, no login, and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Game saves and settings

Your progress, saved games, and settings are stored only on your own device (in your browser/app local storage). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

No analytics, ads, or tracking

The Game contains no third-party advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no social media integrations. We do not build a profile of you and we do not use cookies to track you.

Network use

The Game runs offline and does not transmit any personal data over the internet.

Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect data from children because we do not collect data at all.

Purchases

If you buy the full version of the Game, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Please refer to Google Play's privacy policy for how your purchase information is processed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
